Confidentiality Policy

Last updated: 20.08.2026

This Confidentiality Policy explains how Anhelina Doha, an Individual Entrepreneur registered in Georgia, Identification Number 306548974 ("we", "us", "our"), handles confidential information received from customers and clients in connection with our digital products, consultations, coaching and career-related services.

We recognise that clients may share private, professional, financial, career-related and commercially sensitive information when using our services. We are committed to treating such information confidentially and using it only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services.

1. What We Consider Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" may include information shared with us before, during or after the provision of services, including:

  • ·CVs, résumés and cover letters;
  • ·employment and professional history;
  • ·current or previous employer information;
  • ·salary, compensation and offer information;
  • ·interview details and recruitment processes;
  • ·job-search activity and applications;
  • ·career plans and professional goals;
  • ·internal company information provided by a client;
  • ·personal circumstances discussed during consultations;
  • ·documents, screenshots, messages or correspondence shared with us;
  • ·notes and materials created during consultations;
  • ·business ideas, strategies or other non-public information;
  • ·any other information that a reasonable person would understand to be private or confidential.

Information does not need to be specifically marked "confidential" in order to be treated as confidential under this Policy.

2. How We Use Confidential Information

Confidential Information will be used only where reasonably necessary to:

  • ·provide the product, consultation, coaching or other service requested by you;
  • ·review or improve your CV, LinkedIn profile, applications or other career materials;
  • ·prepare for consultations or interviews;
  • ·provide recommendations and career guidance;
  • ·communicate with you about the services;
  • ·resolve customer-service issues;
  • ·comply with legal, regulatory or accounting obligations; or
  • ·protect our legitimate legal rights where necessary.

We will not disclose your Confidential Information for unrelated purposes without your permission unless disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

3. Client Sessions and Consultations

The content of individual consultations and coaching sessions is treated as confidential.

We will not disclose to third parties:

  • ·what was discussed during your consultation;
  • ·your job-search circumstances;
  • ·your compensation information;
  • ·your employment situation;
  • ·documents submitted for review; or
  • ·personal or professional information disclosed during the service,

except in the circumstances described in this Policy.

Confidentiality under this Policy is a contractual confidentiality commitment and does not create attorney-client privilege, medical privilege, psychotherapist-client privilege or any other legally privileged professional relationship.

4. Employees, Contractors and Service Providers

Where reasonably necessary to provide our services, Confidential Information may be accessible to employees, contractors or professional service providers working with us.

Access will be limited to information reasonably necessary for their role.

Persons processing personal data on our behalf will be subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations where required by applicable law.

We remain committed to taking reasonable measures to ensure that client information is handled confidentially.

5. Third-Party Technology and Service Providers

We may use third-party technology to operate our services, including:

  • ·payment processors;
  • ·website and hosting providers;
  • ·cloud-storage providers;
  • ·email and communication platforms;
  • ·scheduling platforms;
  • ·document-management tools;
  • ·analytics or business-management tools;
  • ·artificial intelligence or productivity tools, where appropriate.

Where client information is processed through third-party services, such processing will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

We aim to limit the information shared with third-party providers to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Payment information is processed through Flitt / Tpay LLC and relevant payment institutions as described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Case Studies, Testimonials and Marketing

We will not publicly identify you as a client or publish identifiable details about your experience, career situation, CV, job search, salary, employer, interview process or results without your permission.

Testimonials containing your:

  • ·name;
  • ·photograph;
  • ·employer;
  • ·job title;
  • ·social media profile; or
  • ·other information that could reasonably identify you

will be published only with your consent.

We may use aggregated or genuinely anonymised information for educational, statistical or marketing purposes where the information cannot reasonably be linked back to you.

For example, we may state that a client received an offer after a particular type of career strategy without revealing information that identifies that client.

7. Recording of Consultations

Consultations will not be audio- or video-recorded by us without informing you in advance and obtaining any consent required by applicable law.

Where a recording is made with your permission, it will be used only for the purposes communicated to you and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Clients may not record, reproduce, publish or distribute consultations or proprietary materials without our prior permission, except where such restriction would be prohibited by applicable law.

8. Exceptions to Confidentiality

Confidential Information may be disclosed where:

  • ·you have expressly authorised the disclosure;
  • ·disclosure is required by Georgian law or another applicable law;
  • ·disclosure is required by a court, regulator or competent public authority;
  • ·disclosure is reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim;
  • ·disclosure is necessary to investigate fraud, unauthorized transactions or misuse of our services;
  • ·the information was already lawfully public through no breach by us; or
  • ·the information was lawfully obtained from another source without a confidentiality obligation.

Where legally permitted and reasonably practicable, we will seek to limit disclosure to the information necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Information You Make Public

This Policy does not apply to information that you independently make publicly available.

For example, information you publicly publish through LinkedIn, a personal website, social media, public portfolio or another public source may no longer be confidential to the extent that it is publicly available.

However, the fact that individual pieces of information are public does not automatically permit us to disclose the confidential content of our communications or consultations with you.

10. Our Intellectual Property and Confidential Materials

Confidentiality obligations apply in both directions where appropriate.

Certain materials provided by us may constitute proprietary or confidential business information, including:

  • ·consultation frameworks;
  • ·internal methodologies;
  • ·templates;
  • ·guides;
  • ·interview materials;
  • ·educational materials;
  • ·strategies;
  • ·worksheets;
  • ·assessment tools;
  • ·unpublished business materials.

Purchase of or access to such materials does not transfer ownership of our intellectual property.

Unless expressly permitted, clients may not reproduce, resell, publish, distribute, share or commercially exploit our proprietary materials.

Further intellectual-property provisions may be contained in our Terms & Conditions.

11. Duration of Confidentiality

Our confidentiality obligations continue during the provision of the services and after the client relationship has ended.

We will not intentionally disclose Confidential Information merely because a consultation, purchase or other contractual relationship has ended.

Information may nevertheless be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, data-protection or legitimate record-keeping purposes, as further described in our Privacy Policy.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical measures designed to protect Confidential Information against:

  • ·unauthorized access;
  • ·unauthorized disclosure;
  • ·accidental loss;
  • ·misuse;
  • ·alteration; and
  • ·destruction.

However, no internet transmission, email service, electronic communication platform or data-storage system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Client Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for ensuring that they are legally permitted to disclose information they provide to us.

You should not provide confidential information belonging to an employer, former employer or another third party if you are prohibited from sharing that information under an employment agreement, NDA, law or other confidentiality obligation.

Where possible, unnecessary third-party personal or confidential information should be removed before documents are submitted to us.

14. Relationship With Our Privacy Policy

This Confidentiality Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

The Privacy Policy explains how personal data are collected, processed, stored, transferred and deleted and describes the rights available to individuals under applicable data-protection law.

Where Confidential Information also constitutes personal data, it will be processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable personal-data-protection legislation.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Confidentiality Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on our website together with its effective date.

Changes will not retroactively remove confidentiality protections that already apply to information previously provided by a client, except where required by law.

16. Governing Law

This Confidentiality Policy is governed by the laws of Georgia.

Nothing in this Policy limits rights or obligations that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.
